Room chart: pick, move and swap

A bar is a booking's coloured strip on a room's row. To use these, open Rooms, then Chart, and hold the key while you click or drag a bar.

Each example below plays by itself when you reach it. Press Play again to watch it again.

Letting go of a bar changes nothing. It opens a window showing what would happen. Nothing moves until you confirm there.

Good to know

  • How many rooms at once. Ctrl and Shift pick up to 8 rooms. The 9th Ctrl pick is refused with a message, and a Shift pick of more than 8 picks nothing. To take more, click the booking once, tick its rooms in the box, and choose Drag them on the chart.
  • What a big pick can do. Moving rooms to other dates has no limit, whether that is the whole booking or rooms split off to a booking of their own. Changing rooms on the same dates, or moving single nights, still takes at most 8 rooms at a time.
  • Parked bookings, ended bookings, bookings at Check-out or Closed, and a guest past their check-out and still in can't be moved. The chart says why.
  • A faint dashed bar (picked, not held) holds nothing yet, so it can't be dragged. Change its dates or rooms on the booking itself. At Negotiation, a booking with a provisional block does have a bar you can drag.
  • Once nights are marked with Alt, dragging a marked night moves them all, with or without Alt.
  • While a guest is in the house, use Extend the stay or Leaving early to change the dates.
  • If the system chose a booking's room, dropping it on another room of the same type asks whether to give it that room.
  • Click a free night, with Switch rooms off, to start an inquiry for that room from that night.
  • Rest the pointer on a bar for a second to see the booking's details. Click a room number to report or review a fault.
  • The examples use made-up bookings, Karma and Sonam, in two colours so you can follow them. Colour by guest on the real chart does the same.
