# Scenario testing — 18 September 2026

Every booking path the hotel can take, run through the new desk (`new_front_end`, port 3002) as a
person at the counter would, with the backend and database checked behind each step. Each fault
found was fixed, re-tested, and committed; this file is the record.

## How it was run

- **The desk, driven like a person.** A Puppeteer driver signs in with the real PINs (front desk
  `1111`, FOM `2222`, GM `3333`), opens the booking, reads the cards, fills fields by their labels
  and clicks buttons by their words. It records every toast, every failed API call (4xx/5xx) and
  every page error, and takes a screenshot at each stop.
- **The backend behind it.** After each step the booking is read back over the API (stage, folio
  lines, balances, timers, trace, communications) and compared with what the desk showed.
- **Test data.** Every test guest is named `ZZTest …` so it can be found and removed. The user's own
  booking ENT-20260918-0002 (rooms 501 and 504) was only ever read, never changed.
- **Test scripts fixed on the way.** `seed-in-house-test-booking.ts` dated its stay from the UTC
  date — a day behind the hotel between midnight and 06:00 Bhutan time — and could not clean up
  once its folio had an invoice; it now dates from the hotel's day and removes the invoice lines
  first. `delete-booking.ts` refused the fixture's `TEST-` booking id.
- **Database:** `legphel_pms_dev2`. **Clock:** the hotel's own day (18 Sep 2026, Bhutan time).

  they are documents handed over in person, so "issued to the guest" still holds; the desk words it
  correctly now (issue 2).
- **"Quote valid" countdown keeps running after Reserve.** The quotation's validity clock is not

  booking). It shows on the side panel through Stay. Cosmetic; left for a decision.
- **Reserve review after a re-entry** lists "Proforma and the answer · not generated" with a green
  "on record" chip (the proforma it counts belongs to the earlier pass).
- **A room left on the day it was entered** still offers "Issue again" for its key.
- **The interim bill and the tax invoice are recorded as sent to a guest with no email** — they
  are handed over at the desk; the answer block still opens a reply window.
- **A released room keeps its key "out"** until check-out collects it (both keys were collected
  correctly at Check-out).
- **A room change sends the guest a new confirmation email** (the re-freeze issues a voucher for
  the new room) — by design; it is also why a change takes ~6 seconds.
- Small wording: "the 1 unstayed night are not billed"; the billing-model card says "The package to
  the account" at Arrival and "to the agent" at Stay; "Received Nu 0.00 of the min threshold Nu
  0.00" on a booking that asked for no advance.
- **Almost no agency or company has an email on file** (1 of 113 agencies, 0 of 9 companies), so
  their invoices go out unemailed until the address is typed at the desk or added in the admin
  console. Deliberately not the guest's address (issue 41).
- **ENT-20260902-0001** (Ngawang Wangdi, at Check-in — not a test booking, left untouched): its
  guest's profile says "verified" but no verification was ever recorded for any stay. After
  issue 49 its check-in will ask for the verification at the desk.
- **Bhutan INC has five identical "Season" packages** from the rate-card migration; the intake
  list shows one (the others are superseded), so the desk is unaffected.
- **The guest's own share gets no paper of its own** — no receipt, no separate tax invoice
  (BE-41 / BE-43, already flagged on the desk). The tax invoice covers the whole stay.
- **Sending an invoice takes about eight seconds** — the PDF is rendered and the email sent inside
  the request.
- **A cross-type room change shows the new price only after the swap** — the panel says the stay
  re-prices, but the difference appears in the toast once it is done. A preview before the click
  would let the FOM quote the guest first.
- **A dispute names no posting** — the charge is described in words (holding one line out of the
  bill is BE-53, as the dialog says). One was raised on a stay with no minibar charge at all.
- **A blocked room-change or extension walk cannot be finished in the product** — issue 59's
  cause is fixed, but any other refusal mid-walk would again need a script.
- **A conference hall cannot be put on a booking from either desk.** The backend route exists
  (`POST /entries/:id/spaces/allocate`, Inquiry to Stay) and the folio, the tabs and the
  settlement all handle a hall once it is on — but no screen calls the route; it was only ever
  verified over HTTP. The intake's "conference" stay even says "the hall is added on the booking".
  Left for the hall work already deferred (PMS-238: halls have no dates and no prices yet).
- **The VIP path cannot be reached.** Nothing sets a guest's VIP tier — no route, no screen —
  and no VIP tiers are configured, so the VIP verification path, the VIP arrival notice and the
  VIP exemptions never apply. Who may grant VIP, and which tiers, is a decision to make first.
- **A no-show's "they're coming late" path is not on the desk.** SIG-S5's deferral (wait for
  written confirmation, then finalise automatically) and reactivation are in the backend and
  finalise through the same code as the FOM's decision, but the desk offers only the immediate
  no-show; the OTA-notification loop a no-show opens has no desk surface. The no-show notice has
  no message record of its own — its send trail is the record (a new message type needs a
  migration).
- **Settling on account waits for the invoice email.** When a settlement issues the party's tax
  invoice, the desk's Take payment waits until the PDF is made and the email sent — several
  seconds on a cold server. Left as it is: sending in the background would let a quick "Send it"
  send the invoice twice.
- **The in-house test fixture prices an extension at nothing.** `seed-in-house-test-booking.ts` creates no quotation,
  so the extension preview had no rates to project and showed Nu 0.00 with "what the guest already paid covers this
  share". Every real booking carries a quote, and the imported legacy bookings (the only others without one) are all
  closed or expired — so real bookings were used for scenarios 28–32.
- **Deleting a booking can leave its room flagged occupied.** `delete-booking.ts` and `repair-stuck-room-states.ts`
  both keep a room's flag when ANY other live claim touches the room, even a future one — the safe direction, but a
  room left OCCUPIED by the deleted guest then needs a manual reset (room 303 in scenario 28). Tooling only.
- **Only the whole booking can be extended.** An extension carries every room of the last night on; one room of a
  party staying longer than the rest is not a separate act (the rest would leave through an early departure per room).
- **Every walk leaves a front-desk handoff behind.** A room change, extension or setup change re-freezes the
  booking, and each re-freeze raises an arrival handoff (H1) that an in-house booking never works. Nothing shows
  them (the Stay step lists housekeeping, kitchen and pre-checkout only), no clock chases them, and check-out
  retires them — left alone.
- **A room vacated mid-stay can be given out again uncleaned.** It reads "Needs cleaning" (departed-dirty) on the
  Rooms page, but nothing records it cleaned — there is no housekeeping step on the desk, and the check-in and
  room-change gates read a separate physical-state field that a departure never changes. Closing a booking is what
  resets its rooms. A housekeeping flow is its own piece of work.
- **Cash receipts mid-stay carry no reference.** A cash settlement at Check-out demands a payment reference (the
  receipt number); a mid-stay interim payment or a Set-up advance in cash does not ask for one. Left as it is — which
  cash takings must carry a receipt number is a policy call.
- **Agents' commission is never raised for a desk booking.** The closure reads the retired agent
  profile; bookings now link a travel agent, which carries no commission rate. Where it would
  fire it records the booking's *outstanding balance* as the commission. Needs a decision on
  where the rate lives (the agency or its rate package) before it is built.
