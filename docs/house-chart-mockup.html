A proposal for Rooms › Chart with two views. Nothing here is saved, and the guests are made up. To try it:
  • By room: drag a bar sideways to move the stay's dates. Drag it up or down to give it that room. Drop it on another booking's room to switch the two.
  • By room type: drag a booking onto another room type to change its type. Drag it sideways to move its dates.

Rooms

Show the house From 30/10/2026 To 12/11/2026

By room shows every room by its number. A booking made by type has a room the system keeps for it, drawn with a dashed edge and the moves mark. It may still move to another room of the same type until Arrival. A room with the pin is fixed: the guest asked for it.

By room type shows each type as one block: how many of its rooms are free each night, and the bookings holding it, with no room numbers until Arrival. Drop a booking on another type to change its type.
