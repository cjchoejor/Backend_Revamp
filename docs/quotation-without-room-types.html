Built on 13 Nov 2026, as decided below. The paper on this page is the one the system now prints for this trip.

For a booking through a travel agent or a company, the papers that go out name how many rooms and who sleeps in each, never the room type. The hotel gives the rooms on arrival from those free that day, so the paper should not promise a type. A walk-in's or direct guest's papers stay as they are, and the desk keeps seeing the room types.

4 · Which papers this reaches

  • Quotation, a stay's own and a trip's: as above.
  • Proforma invoice: it prints the quotation's own table, so it reads the same way (Room 1, Room 2…). Its advance rows do not change.
  • Confirmation voucher: it sets the rooms out like the quotation with no prices, so its lines read "Season Room 1 · 2 adults". Its email names no room type, so it stays as it is.
  • Not changed: the bills after the stay (master bill, tax invoice), which list the rooms the guests actually had; a walk-in's or direct guest's papers; everything the desk shows.
  • No money changes. Only the words on the papers and in the emails.

5 · What was decided

  1. How a room is named: "Room 1 · 2 adults", each room on its own line.
  2. Rooms at different rates: the package each room is priced on goes in bold in front, "Season Room 1 · 2 adults", "Off season Room 2 · 2 adults".
  3. The line at the foot about allotting rooms: left off.
  4. Sending with the room types: never for now. An admin can turn it on under Admin › Channels and replies › "Papers to an agency or a company", for the roles ticked there (the FOM, the GM and an admin to start with). Then the send box offers it, even on a paper already sent once.
  5. A room the desk gave by number keeps its number: "Room 206 · 2 adults".
  6. The trip's emails (quotation, proforma and voucher) list each stay with its own nights, then the nights in all.
