⚠ Known wrong-pull risks — read this first

In simple words: these are the eight places where the number you see can be wrong today. If a price ever looks off, check this list first — the odds are it's one of these.
#What you'd seeWhy it happens
1 Night audit posts a Nu 0.00 room charge; the per-room card on the Stay screen shows total 0 When the room assignment is created at S5, the system re-calculates the room's frozen price — but it only looks at the negotiated rate the operator typed. If none was typed (the quote used the normal hotel rate), it calculates from 0 instead of going back to the quote's real price. lib/hydrate-room-assignment-composition.ts
2 Meal plans add Nu 0 to the price on walk-in / direct bookings Default meal prices only exist on agent rate cards. A booking with no agent has no meal price source, so CP/MAPL pax get counted but multiplied by 0 — unless the operator types negotiated meal rates per room.
3 The agent's meal-plan package rates and child discount % never apply rate_cards.cpRate / mapLunchRate / mapDinnerRate / apRate / cnbPercent are saved in the admin console but no pricing code ever reads them. Only the room base rate + à-la-carte meal add-ons are used.
4 Children (CNB 6-10, under-6) cost the same as adults — no child discount anywhere The child pricing rules (registry.child.mealPricing) only fill an "advisory" info block — they never change the actual total. The CNB count fields only validate occupancy; they don't price anything.
5 Label says “per night” but the number is the whole stay The desk shows quotations.totalAmount. For new composition-priced quotes that column holds the tax-inclusive whole-stay total; for old quotes it was per-night. Same field, two meanings — the label only fits the old one.
6 The old booking-level “Meal plan” dropdown does nothing The desk still sends it, but the backend's input validator has no such field and silently deletes it. Only the per-room composition editor actually prices meals.
7 Agent negotiated an all-in price, guest gets billed more rate_cards.rateIsTaxInclusive exists but the maths to “remove tax from the all-in price first” isn't wired. A 3,400-all-in rate gets 10% + 5% added on top → ~3,927.
8 Final invoice PDF missing the F&B charges Deliberate: the FINAL PDF prints room lines only. F&B / service charges are collected via the folio balance but don't appear on the printed room invoice. Also its service charge + GST are re-calculated at print time from config, not summed from the posted tax lines.

The three origins of a room rate

In simple words: every price in the whole system begins as ONE nightly room rate, and that rate can come from exactly three places. Think of it as a ladder — the system starts at the top and stops at the first rung that applies:

1. A price the operator typed for that specific room (the “negotiated rate” box in the S2 composition editor) — always wins.
2. The agent's / company's contracted price — used when the booking is linked to a travel agent or corporate account that has a rate card.
3. The hotel's own price list (rate plans, adjusted by season) — the default for everyone else.
① Operator-typed per-room rate
typed at S2, stored inquotations.commercialTerms.roomCompositions[].negotiatedRoomRate → copied at S5 intoroom_assignments.negotiatedRoomRate
② Agent / corporate rate card
booking linked viainquiries.travelAgentId / corporateAccountId → rate_cards.roomBaseRate (or the per-room-type override room_type_rate_overrides.roomBaseRate) + meal & bed add-onsrate_cards.breakfastRate / lunchRate / dinnerRate / extraBedRate
lib/agent-rate-resolution.ts — agent rates skip the minimum-selling-rate (MSR) check because they're contractual.
③ Hotel rate plan (default)
rate_plan_registry.baseRate × seasonseason_calendar.rateMultiplier → best plan winsresolvedNightlyRate → discounteffectiveRate = nightly × (1 − %/100)
lib/load-eligible-rate-plans.ts → pricing-pipeline-engine.ts. Discount limits per staff level: registry.discount.actorCeiling.
Taxes (used at every stage)
In simple words: two admin-set percentages get added to almost every charge — service charge first, then GST calculated on (price + service charge). Both live in the admin console under Financial settings.
service chargebilling.serviceChargeRate 10% GSTbilling.salesTaxRate 5% svc = price × 10% · gst = (price + svc) × 5%

The “Indicative Nu 1,500/night” banner
In simple words: this is just the hotel's own price list, shown early so the receptionist has a ballpark to talk from. 2
CNB 6-10 / under-6 counts
In simple words: these boxes say how many children share the room. Today they are used to check the room isn't over-full — they do not change the price at all (wrong-pull risk #4). A child on a meal plan is charged the same meal rate as an adult.
stored inroomCompositions[].cnb6To10Count / cnbUnder6Count → occupancy validation only (Policy 79) — no pricing effect
3
Extra beds
In simple words: each extra bed costs (negotiated bed rate, or the agent card's bed rate, or 0) per night. Note the “or 0”: a walk-in booking with no typed rate adds beds for free. Also: 3+ adults in one room forces at least one extra bed (Policy 78).
extraBedCount × (negotiatedExtraBedRate ?? rate_cards.extraBedRate ?? 0)
4
FOC (free of cost)
In simple words: ticking FOC makes this one room completely free — room, meals, beds, taxes all become 0.00 — while the other rooms still charge normally.
5
Meal plan counts (CP / MAPL / MAPD / AP / Others)
In simple words: these counts are converted into “how many breakfasts / lunches / dinners per night”. CP = breakfast only; MAPL = breakfast+lunch; MAPD = breakfast+dinner; AP = all three. Each meal serving is then priced at (negotiated meal rate, or agent card's à-la-carte rate, or 0). The agent card's package prices (cpRate, apRate…) are never used — risk #3. And with no agent + no typed rates, meals cost 0 — risk #2.
breakfasts/night = CP + MAPL + MAPD + AP (+ Others-breakfast pax) lunches/night = MAPL + AP (+ Others-lunch pax) dinners/night = MAPD + AP (+ Others-dinner pax) meals money = Σ pax × (negotiated ?? rate_cards.mealRate ?? 0)
6
Negotiated room rate
In simple words: if the guest haggled a special price for this room, type it here — it beats the agent rate and the hotel rate list for this room only. Left empty, the system falls back to the agent rate, then the hotel rate plan (see “three origins” above).
7
“Indicative total (per night)” + Quote history amounts
In simple words: both show the same saved number: the quotation's totalAmount column. When the quote was priced per room (the new way) that number is the whole-stay total including taxes — e.g. 1,963.50 = 1,700 room + 170 service + 93.50 GST. The label “per night” is left over from the old system and is wrong for these quotes (risk #5). The screen does no maths — it just prints the stored value.
quotations.totalAmount → apientry.quotations[].totalAmount → displayed verbatim, 2 decimals
HOW totalAmount was written: per-room composition quote → Σ each room's (room + beds + meals) × nights + svc + gst ← STAY total old flat quote → effectiveRate × roomCount ← per NIGHT
8
Discount %
In simple words: the percentage is taken off the nightly room rate first, and then everything is recalculated from that cheaper rate — beds, meals, service charge and GST all shrink with it. Example: 1,700 → 1,530, so the total goes 1,963.50 → 1,767.15. Each staff level has a maximum % they may give (set in /admin/policies); going over it needs a higher level. Applying a discount also throws away any already-generated PDF so the guest can never receive an old price.
ceilingregistry.discount.actorCeiling → effectiveRate = nightly × (1 − %/100) → whole composition re-priced → new quotations.totalAmount, PDF revision bumped

“Received Nu …”
In simple words: the sum of every payment recorded IN on this booking's folio. Each “Record payment” click inserts one row into the payments table; this number is just their total.
Σ payment_records.amount where direction = IN

“required Nu …”
In simple words: the deposit the hotel demands, from an admin setting. It can differ per booking source (OTA vs agent vs walk-in), and group bookings get it multiplied (default ×2). Setting it to 0 means no deposit is demanded — but if the guest pays anyway, the proforma must be dispatched before confirming (rule added 2026-07-28).
advancePayment.thresholds — per inquiries.sourceChannel ?? DEFAULT × registry.groupBooking.advancePaymentBoost when group
16
Advance on the proforma PDF
In simple words: unlike the quotation (always 0), the proforma subtracts what the guest has already paid — same payment rows as pin 14 — so Total Payable is what's still owed.

S4 Confirm — the freeze

In simple words: confirming is the point of no return — the system takes a permanent photocopy of the agreed deal and stores it on the reservation. From here on, everything downstream (night audit, invoices) is supposed to read from this photocopy, not from live settings that might change later.
What gets frozen, and from where
nightly ratereservations.frozenRate ← commercialTerms.effectiveRate (the post-discount per-night room rate — not the stay total)
the whole dealreservations.frozenCommercialTerms ← full terms, including every room's composition and its already-priced totals
who paysreservations.frozenBillingModel ← latest billing_model_transition_records.toModel
Confirmation voucher PDF
In simple words: the voucher lists who sleeps where with which meals — it shows no money. Rows come from the room assignments if they exist; usually at S4 they don't yet, so it falls back to the booking-level adult/child counts with the room number left blank.

The per-room frozen total — wrong-pull risk #1, the big one
In simple words: when a room is assigned, its composition is copied from the accepted quote onto the assignment row, and the room's price is re-calculated — but the recalculation only knows the typed negotiated rate. If the quote used the hotel rate or agent rate (nothing typed), it recalculates from 0 → the card shows Nu 0.00 and the night audit later posts 0.00 room charges. The correct number already exists (it's inside the frozen deal photocopy at reservations.frozenCommercialTerms) — the code just doesn't read it.
roomCompositions[roomId] from accepted quote → room_assignments.* (counts, rates, toggles) → room_assignments.frozenSubtotal / frozenTotal = priced from negotiatedRoomRate ?? 0 ⚠

The nightly room charge
In simple words: every night, the night-audit job posts one room charge per room. For composition bookings it takes the room's frozen stay price and divides by nights (so it's pre-tax); older bookings use the frozen nightly rate from the reservation. This is where risk #1 bites: a 0.00 frozen total → 0.00 nightly charges.
room_assignments.frozenSubtotal ÷ nights fallback reservations.frozenRate → folio_lines.amount (ROOM_CHARGE)
19
A manual charge and its two automatic tax lines
In simple words: when the operator posts a dinner of 500, the system automatically adds two more lines: 10% service charge (50.00) and 5% GST on 550 (27.50). Three lines total — the folio is append-only, nothing is edited in place.
20
Balance due
In simple words: one running total, always recomputed by the server: everything charged, minus everything paid in, plus refunds out, minus write-offs. The screen never adds lines up itself — it prints the server's number.
folios.outstandingBalance = max(0, Σ folio_lines.amount − Σ payments IN + Σ payments OUT − Σ write-offs)

21
Invoice rows
In simple words: only the ROOM charge lines from the folio are printed — one per room per night. The dinner and its taxes from S7 are not on this PDF (risk #8, by instruction: “room bill only”); the guest pays them through the folio balance at settlement.
folio_lines where lineType = ROOM_CHARGE → printed + snapshotted into invoice_lines
22
Service charge & GST on the invoice
In simple words: these are recalculated at print time from the admin settings, applied to the room subtotal — they are NOT the tax lines already sitting on the folio. That's fine for room lines (night audit posts them pre-tax), but it means the invoice's tax figures and the folio's posted tax lines are two different calculations.
23
Total Payable · and the “Take payment & settle” amount
In simple words: the invoice's Total Payable = room subtotal + taxes − advance already paid. The settlement screen, however, charges the folio balance (which includes F&B). Paying it inserts a final payment row; when the balance reaches exactly 0 the folio flips to SETTLED and the room is released to housekeeping.
folios.outstandingBalance → new payment_records row (IN) → balance recomputed → SETTLED at 0
