⚠ Known wrong-pull risks — read this first

In simple words: these are the eight places where the number you see can be wrong today. If a price ever looks off, check this list first — the odds are it's one of these.
#What you'd seeWhy it happens
1 Night audit posts a Nu 0.00 room charge; the per-room card on the Stay screen shows total 0 When the room assignment is created at S5, the system re-calculates the room's frozen price — but it only looks at the negotiated rate the operator typed. If none was typed (the quote used the normal hotel rate), it calculates from 0 instead of going back to the quote's real price. lib/hydrate-room-assignment-composition.ts
2 FIXED 2026-07-30 — was: meal plans and extra beds add Nu 0 on walk-in / direct bookings Bed and meal rates only existed on agent rate cards, so a booking with no agent multiplied every CP/MAPL pax and every extra bed by 0. Now the new house_tariffs table supplies them for any booking without a rate card. Extra bed = 900, seeded from the legacy room.csv. Meal rates are still NULL — the legacy data contains no meal tariff, so walk-in meals price at 0 until an L4 sets them via POST /api/admin/house-tariff.
3 FIXED 2026-07-30 — was: the agent's meal-plan package rates never apply rate_cards.cpRate / mapLunchRate / mapDinnerRate / apRate are now read, via the new mealPlanRates block on the rate resolver. A guest on a plan is charged the plan rate instead of the meals it covers. No price moved for existing agents — all 127 active cards have cpRate == breakfastRate and apRate == bf+ln+dn, so plan rate and fallback are the same number; a real package discount will now be honoured. cnbPercent is still unread — see risk #4.
4 Children (CNB 6-10, under-6) cost the same as adults — no child discount anywhere The child pricing rules (registry.child.mealPricing) only fill an "advisory" info block — they never change the actual total. The CNB count fields only validate occupancy; they don't price anything.
5 Label says “per night” but the number is the whole stay The desk shows quotations.totalAmount. For new composition-priced quotes that column holds the tax-inclusive whole-stay total; for old quotes it was per-night. Same field, two meanings — the label only fits the old one.
6 The old booking-level “Meal plan” dropdown does nothing The desk still sends it, but the backend's input validator has no such field and silently deletes it. Only the per-room composition editor actually prices meals.
7 Agent negotiated an all-in price, guest gets billed more rate_cards.rateIsTaxInclusive exists but the maths to “remove tax from the all-in price first” isn't wired. A 3,400-all-in rate gets 10% + 5% added on top → ~3,927.
8 Final invoice PDF missing the F&B charges Deliberate: the FINAL PDF prints room lines only. F&B / service charges are collected via the folio balance but don't appear on the printed room invoice. Also its service charge + GST are re-calculated at print time from config, not summed from the posted tax lines.

The three origins of a room rate

In simple words: every price in the whole system begins as ONE nightly room rate, and that rate can come from exactly three places. Think of it as a ladder — the system starts at the top and stops at the first rung that applies:

1. A price the operator typed for that specific room (the “negotiated rate” box in the S2 composition editor) — always wins.
2. The agent's / company's contracted price — used when the booking is linked to a travel agent or corporate account that has a rate card.
3. The hotel's own price list (rate plans, adjusted by season) — the default for everyone else.

Add-ons — extra bed & meals (separate from the room rate)
In simple words: the three origins above decide the room price only. Extra beds and meals come from a different place, and which place depends on one thing: does this booking have a travel agent or company attached?

Agent / corporate booking → their rate card's add-on rates. A blank field on the card means the hotel agreed nothing, so it stays free (Nu 0).
Walk-in / direct / OTA → the hotel's own house tariff (added 2026-07-30). Before this existed, these bookings had no add-on source at all, so every bed and every meal was silently free.

The two never mix: a booking with a rate card never falls back to the house tariff.

In simple words: two admin-set percentages get added to almost every charge — service charge first, then GST calculated on (price + service charge). Both live in the admin console under Financial settings.

2
CNB 6-10 / under-6 counts
In simple words: these boxes say how many children share the room. Today they are used to check the room isn't over-full — they do not change the price at all (wrong-pull risk #4). A child on a meal plan is charged the same meal rate as an adult.
stored inroomCompositions[].cnb6To10Count / cnbUnder6Count → occupancy validation only (Policy 79) — no pricing effect
3
Extra beds
In simple words: each extra bed costs (negotiated bed rate, or the agent card's bed rate, or 0) per night. Note the “or 0”: a walk-in booking with no typed rate adds beds for free. Also: 3+ adults in one room forces at least one extra bed (Policy 78).
extraBedCount × (negotiatedExtraBedRate ?? rate_cards.extraBedRate ?? 0)
4
FOC (free of cost)
In simple words: ticking FOC makes this one room completely free — room, meals, beds, taxes all become 0.00 — while the other rooms still charge normally.
5
Meal plan counts (CP / MAPL / MAPD / AP / Others)
In simple words: these counts are converted into “how many breakfasts / lunches / dinners per night”. CP = breakfast only; MAPL = breakfast+lunch; MAPD = breakfast+dinner; AP = all three. Each meal serving is then priced at (negotiated meal rate, or agent card's à-la-carte rate, or 0). The agent card's package prices (cpRate, apRate…) are never used — risk #3. And with no agent + no typed rates, meals cost 0 — risk #2.
breakfasts/night = CP + MAPL + MAPD + AP (+ Others-breakfast pax) lunches/night = MAPL + AP (+ Others-lunch pax) dinners/night = MAPD + AP (+ Others-dinner pax) meals money = Σ pax × (negotiated ?? rate_cards.mealRate ?? 0)
6
Negotiated room rate
In simple words: if the guest haggled a special price for this room, type it here — it beats the agent rate and the hotel rate list for this room only. Left empty, the system falls back to the agent rate, then the hotel rate plan (see “three origins” above).
7
“Indicative total (per night)” + Quote history amounts
In simple words: both show the same saved number: the quotation's totalAmount column. When the quote was priced per room (the new way) that number is the whole-stay total including taxes — e.g. 1,963.50 = 1,700 room + 170 service + 93.50 GST. The label “per night” is left over from the old system and is wrong for these quotes (risk #5). The screen does no maths — it just prints the stored value.
quotations.totalAmount → apientry.quotations[].totalAmount → displayed verbatim, 2 decimals
HOW totalAmount was written: per-room composition quote → Σ each room's (room + beds + meals) × nights + svc + gst ← STAY total old flat quote → effectiveRate × roomCount ← per NIGHT
8
Discount %
In simple words: the percentage is taken off the nightly room rate first, and then everything is recalculated from that cheaper rate — beds, meals, service charge and GST all shrink with it. Example: 1,700 → 1,530, so the total goes 1,963.50 → 1,767.15. Each staff level has a maximum % they may give (set in /admin/policies); going over it needs a higher level. Applying a discount also throws away any already-generated PDF so the guest can never receive an old price.
ceilingregistry.discount.actorCeiling → effectiveRate = nightly × (1 − %/100) → whole composition re-priced → new quotations.totalAmount, PDF revision bumped

The per-room frozen total — wrong-pull risk #1, the big one
In simple words: when a room is assigned, its composition is copied from the accepted quote onto the assignment row, and the room's price is re-calculated — but the recalculation only knows the typed negotiated rate. In simple words: every night, the night-audit job posts one room charge per room. For composition bookings it takes the room's frozen stay price and divides by nights (so it's pre-tax); older bookings use the frozen nightly rate from the reservation. In simple words: when the operator posts a dinner of 500, the system automatically adds two more lines: 10% service charge (50.00) and 5% GST on 550 (27.50). Three lines total — the folio is append-only, nothing is edited in place.

In simple words: these are recalculated at print time from the admin settings, applied to the room subtotal — they are NOT the tax lines already sitting on the folio.
