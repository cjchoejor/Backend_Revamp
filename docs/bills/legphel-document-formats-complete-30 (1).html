GPropagation — what moves when something changes

The question behind this section: if a money receipt changes, does the shift remittance entry change too? The answer turns on a distinction the design depends on. A receipt is never changed — it is voided and reissued. And the shift remittance does not retroactively change either: it lists the void, with its reason, in the receipt range. That is precisely why the range is printed rather than the total alone. The propagation is real, but it is always one of three things — a regeneration, a new instrument, or a register entry. Never an edit.

G1

The three gates

Every propagation question resolves the same way once you know which gates the correction falls behind. Before a gate, correction is free and invisible. After it, the correction becomes an instrument of its own.

OpenFolio liveCharges post and re-post freely. Component bills and the Master Bill are views — they regenerate on demand and no document exists yet to contradict.
Gate 1Folio seal · checkoutStatement content freezes. Reprints reproduce the sealed state with an ordinal. A later charge is a post-stay supply, not a re-opening.
Gate 2Fiscal issueThe tax invoice becomes immutable. Corrections take one of three paths only: adjustment note · VOID + fresh · amended invoice. The 7-day awareness clock starts.
Gate 3Period close · register + filingThe Output Tax Register is sealed and the return asserted. Later adjustments land in the next period, cross-referenced — never backdated into a closed one.

Open · P-01 — Architect ruling requiredWhen a voided receipt is cited on an already-issued tax invoice, the invoice face carries a reference that no longer stands, while the money and the tax are unchanged. Two defensible paths: (i) treat it as a defective non-fiscal particular and issue an amended invoice under r.191 with GST effect Nu. 0.00 — doctrinally clean, but it spends a fiscal serial and re-papers the client for a reference change; (ii) leave the invoice untouched and let the issuance register's replaces-link carry the substitution, on the reasoning that the invoice's statutory particulars are all still correct and the receipt reference is a convenience, not a r.185 requirement. Recommendation: (ii), with a hard rule attached — receipt corrections must be made before invoice issue wherever the folio is still open, so this case stays rare by design. Path (i) reserved for where the payer identity itself changes, which does touch the invoice's substance.

G4

The rule underneath all of it

Documents that are views regenerate. Documents that are instruments never do — a correction to an instrument is another instrument. And anything sealed, whether by folio seal, fiscal issue, or period close, is answered forward in time, never backward. The register is what makes this survivable: every number, live or void, answers for itself.

Which is why the build order in the handbook puts the issuance register before every renderer. A system that can regenerate a statement but cannot explain a voided serial has built the easy half.
