01The stack — how it all fits

Five layers, each constraining the next. Read a document top-down and you can trace every element on it back through this chain. Nothing on any page is a style choice floating free — it is either law, arithmetic, doctrine, psychology, or grid.

Layer 1Bhutan GSTAct + Rules 2026 · 5% from 1 Jan 2026 · no customer exemption
Layer 2Tax modelGST on (rate + 10% service) · ×1.155 · derive down from gross
Layer 330 documents4 legal characters · colour-coded · fiscal never revised
Layer 4Attention doctrineone job · one hero · exceptions get the ink · 8 tests
Layer 5The zone grid10 zones, fixed forever · every document = a configuration

02The zone grid — one template, 30 configurations

Staff read documents spatially before they read them verbally: the price is always bottom-right, the reference always top-right, the policy always above the signature. The grid fixes those positions forever. For development this collapses the build: ten zone components, built once — every document is a zone configuration plus a content mapping.

Z1Masthead — logo centred, name, address · TPN always the last line
Z2Title + character strip (disclaimer directly beneath when required)
Z3Parties — always leftDoc No · Booking Ref · dates · Your ref/PO — always right
Z4Engagement frame — In/Out · Re Check-In rows for split stays
Z5Inventory table — Date · Type/No · Occupants · Meal Plan · Extra Beds · Amount (fixed column order · segment-grouped lines with effective dates)
Z6Money ladder — the hero lives here
Z7Terms & policy — full width, always above signature
Z8Auxiliary — Remittance Block · amenities · please-note (fixed internal order)
Z9Signature — directly under what it acknowledges
Z10DocNo · BookingRef · E&OE · template ver.Page x of y · reprint n
RULE 1Zones never reorder. A moved zone is worse than a missing one — trained eyes read the old position. No document, present or future, moves a zone.
RULE 2Unused zones collapse without residue. No empty frames, no placeholder boxes. (This is also the blank test — a printed blank is a perceived error and a fraud surface.)
RULE 3One renderer component per zone. A document type = zone configuration + content mapping. Build ten components; ship 30 documents.
RULE 4New documents must fit the grid. If one can't, that's an Architect decision, not designer improvisation.
RULE 5Template versioning. Every rendered document records its template version (Z10, small). Templates evolve; issued documents stay reproducible. Template version ≠ document revision.
PRESETSClient types are presets, never formats (D-13). INDIVIDUAL · CORPORATE (+ Your ref/PO, credit terms, accounts contact, optional net-rate column) · GOVERNMENT (corporate + portal ref, own follow-up clock) · AGENCY (rate-silent guest docs). Presets vary zone content; the grid never moves.
VOCABControlled display vocabulary. One canonical name per room type, meal plan, unit, from one config surface. Charge category → component-bill routing. Internal codes never render.

05Tax model, rounding, discounts & deposits

The locked model

GST 5% applies on (rate + 10% service charge) — service charge is consideration inside the GST base, not a parallel tax. Effective inclusive multiplier: ×1.155.

No customer exemption exists. Missions, agencies, NGOs all pay 5% (Act ss.308–309); missions and agencies claim their own refund from DRC (ss.154–155). Two fields support them: customerTaxStatus, refundEntitlementRef — plus a refund-pathway note. No exemption toggle anywhere.

Two directions — never mixed

INCLUSIVE (gross authoritative — current rates)
  GST     = Gross ÷ 21
  Taxable = Gross − GST
  Service = Taxable ÷ 11
  Net     = Taxable − Service

ADDITIVE (net authoritative)
  Service = 0.10 × Net          (2dp/line)
  GST     = 0.05 × (Net+Svc)    (2dp/line)
  Gross   = sum of the rounded parts

Rounding — five rules

1. Line-level, 2dp, at posting — columns sum exactly; a calculator reconciles every face. 2. Each document derives its ladder from its own gross; where a rollup and components ever differ by a chethrum, the rollup (and at settlement, the invoice) governs. 3. Never round an invoice total. 4. Settlement rounding is a payment-side event — not consideration, not taxable, receipt-only. 5. No "round-off" line on any invoice. Render all money as Nu. 27,610.50.

Discounts, FOC, deposits, surcharges (D-12 · D-19 · D-20 · D-22)

Negotiated discount → shown as a line (r.185(5)); GST on the post-discount amount. Promotional/package → the package is the rate; no synthetic rack anchor. FOC room → rendered "Complimentary per group terms", dash in the amount column (GST character of free supplies → advisor, O15). Post-invoice discount → adjustment note within 7 days. Transaction charges → absorbed; "no surcharge on any payment mode" stated; any future surcharge is a taxable line, advisor first. Security deposit ≠ advance → paymentPurpose on every receipt; deposits render "held as security, refundable"; no GST until applied or forfeited (timing → O2). Late payment → none currently applied; credit terms say so.

08Numbering

Two identifiers, two jobs

Booking Ref   LH/26/E/04211   ← mother, constant,
                                on every document
Document No   LH/TI/000140    ← this document,
                                unique, opaque

Non-fiscal: LH/{SER}/{YY}/{NNNNNN}, resets annually. Fiscal (TI, AN): LH/{SER}/{NNNNNN} — no year, continuous, gapless, never reused.

Hard rules

Legacy styles are retired — RES…/INV_… are never generated, printed, or accepted; legacy refs live only as a migration lookup field. No meaning inside numbers — no dates, no reservation refs. No revision markers, ever — revision = a fresh number. Receipts (MR/RR) run gapless with a VOID disposition. Reminders carry no series — a per-invoice R-counter in the dispatch log (D-23).

The allocator (build item)

MAX+1, UUIDs, and DB sequences all fail (sequences gap on rollback by design). Required: a DocumentSeries row per series, allocation inside the issue transaction under a row lock, no allocation at draft time, and a permanent DocumentIssuanceRecord per allocation — number, type, entity, actor, timestamp, disposition ∈ {ISSUED, VOID, CANCELLED, SUPERSEDED}. The register is what makes a VOID a non-gap: the sequence is unbroken and every number answers for itself.

10Compliance — the ten that matter most

The full 21-row matrix is DFG-001 §3. These are the ten a developer must never break; several are hard gates, not advisories.

r.185(6) GST statedThe ladder and the sentence: "GST of Nu. X is included in the total consideration of Nu. Y." Missing sentence blocks issue.

r.264(4) + s.265(6) price displayInclusive-led everywhere; the PI carries "not a tax invoice" and GST expected — the two words that keep pre-supply documents lawful.

11The eight acceptance tests

Design review is a checklist, not an opinion. A document ships only when it passes all eight.

TestPass condition
1 · Ten-secondA first-time reader states the document's identity, its one action, and the hero figure within ten seconds.
2 · CalculatorEvery printed column and ladder reconciles on a pocket calculator.
3 · SquintBlurred at arm's length, it cannot be mistaken for its confusable neighbour (invoice ≠ statement ≠ proforma).
4 · PhotocopyOne monochrome copy preserves every distinction that matters — never colour alone.
5 · FoldZ-folded, the top third identifies the document, the reference, and the money.
6 · BlankNo unfilled label prints anywhere.
7 · PatternAt most one deliberate pattern violation — and it is the thing that must not be missed.
8 · PhoneZoomed to width on a phone, identity and hero are legible without pinching or horizontal scrolling — the PDF remains the document; the phone is where it is first read.

12Build checklist

The implementation surface this system asks of the codebase, in a sensible build order.

Ten zone components + document configurations. One renderer per zone (Z1–Z10); each of the 30 documents is a configuration (occupancy + content mapping). Unused zones collapse without residue.
Controlled vocabulary configuration. Canonical names for room types, meal plans, units; charge-category → component-bill routing table. Documents read labels from here only; internal codes cannot reach the render layer.
Client-type presets (D-13). INDIVIDUAL / CORPORATE / GOVERNMENT / AGENCY as configuration varying zone content — Your ref/PO, credit terms, accounts contact, net-rate column, rate-silence. Never a second template.
Tax engine per §05. Configurable rates with effective dates; both derivation directions; line-level 2dp; the GST sentence generated from the ladder, never typed. Discount lines, FOC rendering, and deposit purpose per §4.5 of the guideline.
Series allocator + issuance register. In-transaction, row-locked, issue-time allocation; dispositions ISSUED / VOID / CANCELLED / SUPERSEDED; register append-only and permanent.
Document lifecycle states. DRAFT → ISSUED → DISPATCHED → ACKNOWLEDGED → terminal. DRAFT renders a watermark and no serial; the serial exists only from ISSUED.
Validation gates at issue. Supplier TPN block · customer TPN (registered) or identity ref (unregistered > 50k) · GST sentence · no blank labels · cash-ceiling check at payment capture · receipt purpose captured (advance / deposit / settlement / recovery).
Timers. Adjustment-note 7-day clock from awareness, with escalation before expiry · reissue-request 15-day SLA · validity/due-date timers wired to the printed dates.
Receivables follow-up engine (D-23/24). Per-invoice reminder counter (R1, R2 …) writing to the dispatch log; cadence ladder per client preset (government on its own clock); aged SoA view (buckets, days overdue, total outstanding); Dues Demand Notice generation carrying the reminder history as its evidence line.
Dispatch & acknowledgement log. Per document: channel, timestamps, delivery/read state, acknowledgement — powers reminders and the SLA timers. WhatsApp templates per DFG-001 §12.2 (Meta-approved, positional variables, read-only once approved).
Template versioning. Every render records its template version (Z10). Template update ≠ document revision; issued documents stay reproducible from their own version.
File naming & retrieval. LH-TI-000140.pdf style; retrieval by any identifier in seconds — the 15-day SLA is a retrieval problem.
